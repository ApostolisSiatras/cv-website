Research note · Data governance

Governance before data sharing.

A data-sharing initiative needs a clear answer to who decides, who benefits, and who remains accountable.

Apostolis Siatras  ·  20 September 2026

Citizen-generated data can support useful research and public decisions. Making those data available also creates practical questions about access, consent, and the community’s continuing authority.

The Data cooperatives starter kit, coauthored with Janick Spycher, Edy Portmann, and Anastasija Nikiforova, approaches this as a governance problem. It brings together instruments such as bylaws, consent mechanisms, data-use agreements, assurance, and quality control.

Make weaknesses visible

The accompanying fuzzy evaluation examines stewardship across six dimensions. Its aggregation approach reduces the extent to which strong performance in one area can compensate for a serious weakness elsewhere.

The paper illustrates the approach with a worked example and sensitivity analysis. These demonstrate its evaluation logic; they are not evidence that every real-world cooperative will achieve the same outcomes.
