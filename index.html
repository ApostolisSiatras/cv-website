Beyond a single discipline.

Where privacy engineering meets the humanities.

Master’s thesis · E-Government

GDPR Assessor

My master’s research focused on a platform for assessing GDPR-related privacy implications from the way a scenario is articulated. The work connects the language used to describe a situation with the privacy questions that description raises.

Research focus

The central concern is how descriptions of data use can inform a structured assessment of privacy implications. This brings together my interests in privacy engineering, language, and practical digital tools.

Developed in the context of my MSc in E-Government at the University of the Aegean.

A little context.

The path that brought these interests together.

Before my BSc, I completed a BA in Music Theory at Musicart Conservatory in Kozani. Music is an earlier chapter of the same interest in culture and expression that runs through my research today.

I am a doctoral researcher at the Thapar School of Liberal Arts and Sciences (TSLAS), Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, India. I hold a BSc in Cultural Technology and Communication and an MSc in E-Government from the University of the Aegean. My work brings together privacy engineering, AI, and the governance of data.

I have contributed to European projects through web development, anonymization algorithms, simulations, and testing the limits of cybersecurity platforms. My master’s work on the GDPR Assessor and my experiments with the Lesbos dialect extend these interests to privacy assessment and cultural language technology. My publications explore the risks of language-model profiling, healthcare data anonymization, and governance for citizen data initiatives.

My industry experience includes work as a Junior Researcher and Developer at PRIVACT and as Technical Staff at Cyberalytics. View my corporate experience.

I am associated with the PrivaSI Laboratory at the University of the Aegean and am listed as an external collaborator in the University of Fribourg’s computational ethics research.
